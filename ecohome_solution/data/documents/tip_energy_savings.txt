Everyday energy-saving tips

Shift flexible loads such as dishwashers, laundry, and EV charging away from high-price periods when the household schedule permits. Unplugging idle equipment has a smaller but useful effect; prioritize persistent loads identified in the home's own usage history.

Savings depend on device energy, actual tariff periods, and whether solar would otherwise be exported. Compare the estimated cost of the proposed schedule with a clearly stated baseline, and treat forecasts as estimates rather than guaranteed bills.
