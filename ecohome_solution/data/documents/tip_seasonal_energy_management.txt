Seasonal energy management

In hot weather, close blinds on sun-facing windows during the hottest hours and use fans to improve comfort before lowering the thermostat. In cold weather, use daylight for passive warmth, close curtains after sunset, and check weather stripping around doors and windows.

Revisit schedules when daylight length and outdoor temperatures change. A schedule optimized for summer solar production may miss winter's shorter generation window. Base changes on measured use and the current forecast, and retain safe temperature limits for people, pets, and pipes.
